## Short Bio

**Alexandre Trilla** graduated from La Salle - Ramon Llull University in Barcelona with a M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering in 2008, a M.Sc. in Information Technology Management in 2010, and a Ph.D. in 2024. He has an academic research background in Spoken Language Processing, and an industrial research background in Prognostics and Health Management. With 20 years of work experience he has consistently delivered many added-value solutions and authored several publications (available here) in technical conferences and scientific journals. At present, Dr. Trilla is a Senior Data Scientist, R&D Project Manager and Internal Trainer at Alstom, leading the development and deployment of Predictive Maintenance products for the railway business. His research interests include Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Causal Inference.

### Teaching

#### Causal Inference for Data Science (and Decision-Making)

- Trilla, A., 2025. Overview: Introduction to the science of Causality, mainly centered around Predictive Maintenance and Troubleshooting. Data are currently being collected from many different environments such as signaling, rolling stock or services, but oftentimes the engineering teams lack the ability to interpret them and get actionable insights. In light of this uncertainty, Causal Inference provides the statistical method to address these concerns by leveraging both experience and data for the purposes of explaining what will happen (any why) if action is taken (in contrast to predicting previous passively collected observations).

#### Data Analysis for Prognostics and Health Management

- Trilla, A., 2019. Overview: Introduction to the field of Machine Learning-based data analytics for railway Predictive Maintenance. This short course has been designed for a technical audience, including both individual contributors as well as related industrial management roles.
